A bit about me

This could start with “I am a Systems Engineer…”, but that is just my college degree, and I have grown a lot since. So instead: I am a human being who happens to have that degree.

I have worked in tech since 2016 (web marketing, e-commerce, video games, big tech), teaching great people and learning from others. I keep learning every day about technology, about life and about being a better person.

I lead teams, mentor, teach and speak at conferences and bootcamps. I am also an aspiring voice actor, (slowly) learning about content creation.

Want to know more about me, hire me for an event, support my projects or just have a virtual chat? Feel free to contact me.

I hope you have a great day, and if this one is not, tomorrow you will get another chance.
